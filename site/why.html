Straight talk

The questions worth asking any credit repair company

Ask us these. Ask everyone else too — the answers tell you a lot.

"Can you guarantee my score will go up?"

Our answer: no. Nobody can. The credit bureaus decide every dispute outcome, and your results depend on what's actually on your reports. Any company that guarantees a number is either guessing or lying.

"Will you remove accurate negative items?"

Our answer: no. Accurate, timely, verifiable information stays on your report for the period the law allows. We challenge what appears inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable — that's the actual work.

"Am I locked into a contract?"

Our answer: no. It's month-to-month with no setup fee. Cancel whenever you like, without penalty and without a phone-call gauntlet.

"Could I just do this myself?"

Our answer: yes, and for free. You have the right to dispute directly with the bureaus at no cost. We're here for people who want the expertise and the follow-through handled for them — not because you legally need us.
