About us

People first, paperwork second

CoreWave Credit exists for one reason: to give people a clear, honest path through a process that usually feels confusing and out of reach.

Our mission

Credit shouldn't be a mystery

Most people first learn their credit score matters at the worst possible moment — a denied application, a higher interest rate, a lease that falls through. By then the report has already made the decision for them.

We think that's backwards. Your credit report is a document about you, and you have the legal right to see it, understand it, and challenge anything on it that's inaccurate, incomplete, or unverifiable. Most people simply never get shown how.

That's the work. We review your three-bureau reports line by line, build a strategy around what we find, handle the disputes properly, and tell you what's happening in plain English every step of the way.

Our story

Why we started CoreWave Credit

We've seen how much a credit score quietly decides in a person's life — and how few people are ever given a straight explanation of how it works.

CoreWave Credit was built around a simple observation: the credit repair industry has a reputation problem, and it earned it. Too many companies over-promise, charge for results they can't deliver, and leave clients in the dark about what's actually being done on their behalf.

We wanted to run the opposite kind of business. That means being upfront that we cannot remove accurate information, that no one can promise you a specific score, and that the credit bureaus — not us — make the final call on every dispute. What we can promise is that the work gets done thoroughly, lawfully, and visibly.

It also means treating this as a relationship rather than a transaction. Our clients aren't case numbers. They're people trying to buy a first home, refinance a car, start a business, or just stop being penalized for something that isn't even theirs.

What we value

How we work

Three principles that shape every client relationship.

Integrity over urgency

We'd rather tell you credit repair isn't the right fit than sell you a plan that won't help. Honest expectations from the first conversation.

Patience with the process

Disputes run on the bureaus' timelines, not ours. We work steadily and keep you posted rather than promising overnight miracles.

Education, not dependence

Our goal is that you leave understanding your credit well enough that you don't need us again. We'll teach you what we're doing and why.

A note on what credit repair is — and isn't

Everything we do, you have the legal right to do yourself for free by contacting the credit bureaus directly. We're not a shortcut around the law or a way to erase a legitimate credit history. What we provide is expertise, organization, and follow-through for people who'd rather not navigate it alone. If that's not worth the monthly fee to you, we'd genuinely rather you kept your money.
